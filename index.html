I'm a postdoc in the CoreSec group at TU Graz, where I did my PhD with Daniel Gruss. I work on low-level systems security, mainly side channels and microarchitectural attacks on CPUs, trusted execution environments like Intel TDX, and hypervisors. I also teach operating systems, system-level programming, and hypervisor development.
